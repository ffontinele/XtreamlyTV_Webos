# XtreamlyTV 0.6.0

## Cross-platform TV overhaul

Version 0.6.0 aligns the native Android TV application with the finalized webOS experience while preserving platform-native playback and remote behavior.

### Favorites workspace

- Keeps **My Groups** at the top of Favorites and shows one uniform content grid for the selected group.
- Removes Recently Watched Favorites; watch history remains on Home.
- Adds explicit **Edit groups** and **Reorder items** workflows instead of hidden long-press modes.
- Allows every group, including All Favorites, Live TV, Movies, and Series, to be renamed, recolored, re-iconed, hidden, shown, and reordered.
- Keeps built-in membership automatic and hides the item-selection pane while editing built-in groups.
- Allows item ordering for every group except the aggregate All Favorites group.
- Persists group visibility, built-in appearance overrides, group order, and per-group item order locally on each TV.

### Navigation and history

- Restores the originating area, category or group, exact content card, and virtual-grid position after closing playback or a detail screen.
- Selecting a catalog category or Favorites group always starts on the first content card rather than the search field or sidebar.
- Home presents separate **Continue watching Live TV**, **Continue watching Movies**, and **Continue watching Series** rails.
- Series details retain the selected season and return to the previously focused episode after playback.

### Android TV startup

- Shows the native XtreamlyTV launch window immediately on cold start.
- Holds the platform splash until Compose has produced its first branded frame, preventing a blank launcher-to-app transition.
- Continues with the in-app XtreamlyTV loading screen while encrypted credentials and provider authentication are processed.

## Platform notes

- Android TV stores the new visibility, appearance, ordering, history, and focus state in device-local preferences and native Compose state.
- webOS and Android TV now share the same product behavior while retaining native HTML media and Media3 playback implementations respectively.
- Favorites and navigation state remain device-local; v0.6.0 does not introduce account synchronization between televisions.

### Release-candidate display polish

- Uses a transparent vector XtreamlyTV lockup inside both applications while leaving platform launcher artwork unchanged.
- Gives Android category rails whole-pixel row sizing and safe focus-border padding.
- Makes D-pad movement between category rails and content grids explicit and repeatable.
- Fits complete card rows into Android catalog and Favorites viewports and aligns a row whenever focus would otherwise be clipped.
- Keeps Series Details actions visible and fits complete episode rows into the remaining viewport.

### Final regression cleanup

- Uses the exact launcher-icon X artwork for every in-app brand lockup and splash surface.
- Fits complete Movie and Series artwork inside poster cards on webOS and Android TV.
- Snaps Android Live TV browsing to complete 4×4 pages and category browsing to complete ten-button pages.
- Makes Home hero-to-shortcut and catalog category-to-card focus routes deterministic.
- Preserves the category page while switching categories and returns Left from the first card column to the selected category.
